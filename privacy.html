Privacy Policy

Shorts Uploader · Last updated: October 15, 2026

What the app is

Shorts Uploader is a private tool that its owner runs on their own computer to upload short videos to their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel and set their title, description, tags and publication time.
  • Basic channel information (channel ID and name), used only to confirm that uploads go to the intended channel.
  • Public statistics of the uploaded videos (views, likes, comments).

How the data is stored and shared

OAuth tokens and video statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the app and are not sold or shared with third parties. The app does not collect data about viewers or other YouTube users.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Locally stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked or when the owner removes them.
